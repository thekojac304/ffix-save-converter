## Blockers and open items

* PS1 location of the countdown timer (`timeCounter`, `timerControl`, `timerDisplay`) is not proven; the two candidate regions (0x182-0x19F, 0x1AC-0x1BF) are zero in all 54 fixtures, matching the default.
* PS1 config (`cfg`: ATB mode, message speed, window colour...) is not located; settings come from a PC template slot or documented defaults.
* PS1-only regions with unknown meaning and no PC counterpart: 0x0174-0x017F (0x178 = per-card save counter), 0x09C0-0x09CF, 0x0EF2-0x0F1F (0xEF2-0xEF5 likely btl_result/btl_flag/btl_rain/steiner_state by RAM order), 0x1160-0x1177, 0x13F4-0x13FD and per-player +0x22-0x23, +0x48-0x57. PS1->PC lists them in the report; PC->PS1 zero-fills them unless a PS1 save of the same playthrough is given (`--ps1-base`) or the model came from PS1. Whether the PS1 game accepts zeros there is **not yet emulator-verified**.
* Still 'inferred': `summon_flag` (bitmask grows as eidolons are seen; PC fixtures always 0), `mode/prevMode`, playtime scaling, Tetra Master W/L/D.
* Disc boundaries for PC->PS1 come from observed saves; scenario values inside the gaps (3881-3899, 6961-6989, 10981-10994) require `--disc`.
* PAL and Japanese saves were not available; PAL is accepted with a warning, Japanese is rejected.

## Reverse direction (PC -> PS1)

Every row above is applied in reverse by `src/ffix_conv/ps1_writer.py`. Non-trivial inverses: party 255 -> 9; info packed into the u32 bitfield (values over the bit widths are rejected); PC `player_bonus` 0 -> (level-1)*5 (+10..99 for serial 10/11), i.e. what the PC game itself would use; `wldMapNo/wldLocNo` 0/0 -> PS1 initial 9009/68; cards packed into the first records of 105, remaining records `FF 00 00 00 00 00`; playtime seconds -> frames; disc mask from scenario; names re-encoded with the PS1 text table (characters outside it are rejected). PC-only data (settings, achievements, world camera, timer, gStepCount, card side/cpoint) cannot be represented and is listed in the report.
